Verros Sans and Verros Mono are renamed, Latin-subset WOFF2 builds of two SIL Open Font License 1.1 fonts
(see LICENSE.txt). They are renamed because the OFL reserves the original font names for unmodified builds.

Barlow (Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors, https://github.com/jpt/barlow)
  Barlow-Light.ttf     -> verros-sans-300.woff2
  Barlow-Regular.ttf   -> verros-sans-400.woff2
  Barlow-Medium.ttf    -> verros-sans-500.woff2
  Barlow-SemiBold.ttf  -> verros-sans-600.woff2
  Barlow-Bold.ttf      -> verros-sans-700.woff2

JetBrains Mono (Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors, https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono)
  JetBrainsMono[wght].ttf -> verros-mono.woff2  (variable, weight axis limited to 400-700)

Changes: subset to Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, general punctuation, euro, trademark and arrows; converted to
WOFF2; family names changed. Glyph outlines are unmodified.
