Original file              -> this file
Archivo[wdth,wght].ttf     -> verros-sans.woff2

Changes: weight axis limited to 300-800 and width axis to 100-125; subset to Latin-1, Latin Extended-A,
general punctuation, euro, trademark and arrows; converted to WOFF2; family name changed to "Verros Sans".
Glyph outlines are unmodified.
